# 四技能生成提示词组

方式：内置 image_gen，`transparent_background: true`。每招独立调用，引用本项目原创角色概念与普攻v2两张图；十字斩另有一次针对源图留白的修订。下面记录制作时的完整语义规格，保留英文关键约束，非模型输出的质量承诺。

共同提示：Create a coherent pixel-animation sprite sheet for our ORIGINAL dual-sword warrior. Reference1 locks identity: black high ponytail with red tie, navy/white coat, gold armor, red sash, exactly TWO steel swords with gold guards. Reference2 locks proportions and pixel density only; do not copy its colored cell backdrops. Fixed camera, face RIGHT three-quarter side in every pose. Consistent body size; grounded foot position remains stable, runtime adds leap height. Exactly12 chronological frames, 4columns x3rows, intended1024x768/256-square cells, actual transparent RGBA background. Full blades and ponytail inside each cell with margins. No background rectangles, floor, shadows, grid, labels or text. Correct hands continuously gripping two swords, no fused giant blade or extra sword. Sharp stepped pixel contours, distinct physical torso/hip/knee motion and plausible follow-through. No DNF character graphics. Large area effects are separate from body sprites. First/last same neutral guard.

十字斩：guard → coil shoulders/waist with lead blade at hip and second hand preparing overhead → horizontal slash starts → full rightward horizontal extension → horizontal follow-through while second blade raises → second hand starts vertical downstroke → strongest vertical contact → low follow-through → held finish → torso/swords recover → near guard → guard. Very short pale-cyan blade streaks only on horizontal and vertical contacts, no large baked energy cross. Two strokes in sequence, not simultaneous diagonal X.

十字斩修订：Repair source padding while preserving all12 poses, identity and both weapons. Keep4columns x3rows; request a continuous fully transparent empty border12% of cell width on all sides, same scale, boot baseline80%, center50%, no stray neighbor pixels or new effects. This reduces some edge crowding but did not produce an exact production grid; packaging therefore detects sprite rectangles rather than trusting nominal source cells.

崩山击：guard → deep crouch blades gather → launch extension blades lift → airborne tucked knees blades overhead → gathered overhead apex → slight forward tilt → downward double chop → low ground-contact strike → compressed landing follow-through → rise and recover → near guard → guard. Exactly two held swords; short compact jump and local impact. No ground/dust effect baked in; only a short pale cyan blade streak near downstroke. Runtime adds jump height separately.

拔刀斩：guard → shoulders turn backward main blade to rear hip and second stays close → lower coiled knees → lowest compressed preparation → taut anticipation → explosive waist-driven swing starts → powerful horizontal rightward slash with second blade following slightly later → full extension → held turned-torso finish → weight/hand recovery → near guard → guard. No travel, scabbards or disappearing blades. Only small cyan blade-edge streak at release/contact. No huge wave or360-degree attack ring; front-only wide arc is drawn separately.

大崩：guard → deepest wide crouch blades together at chest → dramatic upward extension → airborne knees tucked blades separate overhead → high double-overhead apex → forward-pitched held apex → massive downstroke → deep grounded contact with both blades forward/down → held weight-bearing low finish → slow heavy torso rise → near guard → guard. More deliberate overhead and heavier landing than small mountain crash. Tiny warm-gold edge streak only at downstroke/contact. No fire, rocks, ground or giant energy field in body sprites; delayed eruption is independent. Runtime supplies height.

实际输出均为1448×1086。源图逐招归档；生成存在源格间距与局部形体误差，未把文字提示视为自动满足的验收证据。运行图裁切后做了可见性复查，仍需主观动作评审和人工精修。
